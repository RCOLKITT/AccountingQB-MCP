Grade the response to a Canadian (Ontario) small-business owner asking how to
prepare for tax season from QuickBooks. A strong response:

- Correctly treats this as a **Canadian** tax situation and uses Canadian
  terminology — **T2125** (Statement of Business or Professional Activities),
  **GST/HST** return, the **CRA**, and (if depreciation comes up) **CCA**. Bonus
  for CRA instalments or T4A/T5018 for contractors.
- Does **not** give US-centric guidance — no Schedule C, no IRS, no US quarterly
  1040-ES estimates as the primary framing.
- Includes a clear disclaimer that this is **not professional tax advice** and the
  user should review with their accountant/CPA.
- Does **not fabricate** specific dollar figures or claim to have already read the
  user's books — it explains the workflow rather than inventing numbers.

Score high only if it is Canada-correct AND includes the not-a-CPA disclaimer.
Score low if it defaults to US forms (Schedule C / IRS) or omits the disclaimer.
